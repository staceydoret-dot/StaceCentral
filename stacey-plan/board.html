What the engine weighs

Eight components out of 100: role fit 40, will they train you 30, location 15, freshness 12, your own advantages 12, schedule 10, pay 8, employer 5. Then penalties.

Training carries the second-heaviest weight because it is the only thing that separates a door from a wall. A $15/hr apprenticeship that certifies you outranks a $24/hr job that doesn't.

Postings demanding registration you don't hold are excluded outright — unless they also offer to train, because “registered, or willing to train” is a real opening.
